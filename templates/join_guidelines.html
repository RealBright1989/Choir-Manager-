Membership Guidelines

Review each step carefully before proceeding to registration

1

Personal Information

Provide your accurate legal details

▼
  • Full Name — Enter your legal first and last name as they appear on official documents
  • Date of Birth — Used for age verification and membership records
  • State of Origin & LGA — Helps us understand our diverse community
⚠ Required fields marked with *
2

Contact Details

Valid phone number required for verification

▼
  • Phone Number — You will receive a one-time verification code via SMS. A valid, active number is required.
  • Email Address — Optional but recommended for receiving newsletters and official communications
🔒 Phone verification ensures real-person registration
3

Additional Information

Optional details to help organize sections

▼
  • Academic Qualification — Helps us understand the educational background of our members
  • NIN Number — National Identification Number for official records (optional)
✓ These fields are optional
4

Privacy & Data Protection

How we handle your information

▼
  • Your personal data is stored securely and used only for choir management purposes
  • We do not share your information with third parties without your consent
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time
  • Data is protected in accordance with applicable privacy regulations
5

Code of Conduct

Uphold our choir values and standards

▼
  • Attendance — Attend rehearsals and events punctually. Notify leadership in advance if unable to attend
  • Dues — Pay membership dues promptly as they fund our activities and operations
  • Respect — Treat fellow members, conductors, and leadership with courtesy and professionalism
  • Participation — Engage actively and contribute positively to the choir's growth
6

Complete Your Registration

Final steps to become a member

▼
  • Verify your phone number with the OTP code sent via SMS
  • Review all information before submitting — edits after submission require admin approval
  • You will receive a confirmation once your registration is processed
⏱ Total process takes approximately 3-5 minutes
