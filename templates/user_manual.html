User Manual

Complete guide to using the {{ settings.choir_name }} Management System.

Getting Started

Login with your username and password. Default admin credentials: admin / admin123. Change your password after first login.

Dashboard

After login, the Dashboard shows summary cards: total members, revenue collected, attendance rate, songs count, and recent payments. Use it to get a quick overview of your choir's activities.

Members

  • Add Member: Click "Add Member" and fill in name, phone, email, section (Soprano/Alto/Tenor/Bass), join date, address, and notes.
  • Edit/Delete: Use the buttons in the table. Deleting a member also removes their payment and attendance records.
  • Search: Type a name or phone number in the search box to filter members.
  • Member Detail: Click a member's name to view their full profile, payment history, and attendance records.

Attendance

  • Select a date and mark each member as Present, Absent, or Excused.
  • Click "Save Attendance" to record. You can revisit past dates to view or edit attendance.

Finance / Payments

  • Record Payment: Select a member, enter amount, date, and what the payment is for (e.g. "January dues").
  • Filter: Use the dropdown to view payments for a specific member.
  • Member Totals: The table at the bottom shows total paid per member.

Songs

  • Add Song: Enter title, composer, lyrics, and optionally upload an audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG).
  • Play: Click "Play" to listen to uploaded audio in your browser.

Reports & Export

  • View section breakdown, attendance summary, and monthly financial reports.
  • Export: Download Members, Payments, or Attendance as CSV files.
  • Full Backup: Download a complete CSV backup of all data.

Settings

  • Change the choir name, currency symbol, and default dues amount.
  • Configure social media links for Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

User Management (Admin only)

  • Admins can create, manage, and delete system users.
  • Roles: Admin (full access), Treasurer (finance focus), Viewer (read-only).

Social Links

View and click through to your choir's social media pages. Configure the URLs in Settings.

Tip: Always log out when finished, especially on shared computers.
